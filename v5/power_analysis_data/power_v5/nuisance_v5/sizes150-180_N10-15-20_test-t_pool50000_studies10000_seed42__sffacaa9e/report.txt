  on average (half shift: 0.106). The largest costs come from fatigue in the

  places under half shift (difference there +0.010, SE 0.004; beyond 2 SE).
  The six separate costs add up to only 73% (planning) and 85% (half shift) of
  the total drop: the nuisances compound, so together they cost more than the

2. WHAT WAS DONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Paired t-test (the registered primary test), power only, planning and half
  shift, block sizes 150 and 180, N = 10, 15, 20, both features; pair-specific
  preferences off. Eight conditions: all nuisances at script 7's defaults;
  each of the six switched off on its own (to script 7's 'off' value); no
  nuisances. Power returned by a nuisance = power with it off minus power with
  all defaults. Ranked by the unweighted average over the 12 cells. Script 7's
  person model, pools of 50,000 people, 10,000 studies per cell, SE from 20
  sub-pools.
  Common random numbers: within an effect and block size, all eight conditions
  use the same random draws and the same study draws, so each recovery is a
  paired difference and its SE is that of the difference. 'Scene dependence
  off' is SD x 1e-09 rather than exactly 0, because script 7 draws the scene
  deviations only when they are on and a 0 would shift every later draw; at x
  1e-9 the deviations are numerically nothing. Changing the second-block lapse
  changes catch failures and so who is excluded: in the block-order and
  no-nuisance conditions some studies sample different people (section 5),
  which makes those two paired differences somewhat noisier.

4. DO THE SIX ADD UP?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Total drop = power with no nuisances minus power with all defaults. If the
  nuisances acted independently, the six recoveries would sum to it.
  'Interacting' if the sum differs from the total by more than 2 SE and by
  more than 0.01.
  planning    total drop 0.0948 (SE 0.0040); sum of the six 0.0695 (SE 0.0068);
              sum minus total -0.0253 (SE 0.0060); ratio 0.73 -> INTERACTING
  half shift  total drop 0.1058 (SE 0.0049); sum of the six 0.0900 (SE 0.0078);
              sum minus total -0.0158 (SE 0.0062); ratio 0.85 -> INTERACTING
  A ratio above 1 means switching nuisances off one at a time returns more
  power in total than switching them all off together (they overlap); below 1,
  that they compound (each costs more in the presence of the others).
