7. NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Feasibility of counts is exact for the bins and quotas as set (v4's fixed edges,
  trials spread evenly over usable bins). 'without bin quotas' in section 3 shows
  whether a shortfall is the quotas' doing.
* A solver time limit is reported as UNDECIDED, never as infeasible.
* The draws are one way of building matched sets, not the build step itself; the
  build step (script 3) can use the same procedure with its own settings.
* Consistency repeats reuse a block's own pairs and need no capacity; they are not
  drawn here.
