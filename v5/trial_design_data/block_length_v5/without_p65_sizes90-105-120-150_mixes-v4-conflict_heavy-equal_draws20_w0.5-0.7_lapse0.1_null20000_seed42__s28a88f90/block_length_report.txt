10. NOTES AND LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Power is a first estimate under the stated person model. The shift sizes, the taste
  spread, the share of responders and the lapse rate are assumptions, not estimates;
  section 5 shows how far the answer moves when they change.
* Each weight is tested on its own at alpha, uncorrected. A corrected or joint primary
  test would need the power read from 'both' or recomputed.
* Block order is not modelled: an order effect would add to the difference in half
  the people and subtract in the other half, widening its spread.
* Taste is assumed stable across blocks. Taste that differed between blocks would
  add its own variance to every difference.
* The session length is approximate: the per-trial time is v4's assumption, untested.
