WHAT THIS IS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The registered 5% false-claim rate for H1 and H2 assumes that people without the
memory effect have tastes centred on zero. Here their tastes are centred on mu,
in the predicted direction, and the registered thresholds and (k, m) are applied
unchanged. Claim rates are exact given the measured per-person rates; their
Monte Carlo SE is carried through (se_* columns, *_upper = point + 1.96 SE).

Thresholds: reproduction checks use the computed ones; everything else the
registered (rounded) ones.

NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Claim rates are exact given the per-person rates measured on 20,000 simulated
  people per pool. 'either' and 'both' are exact given the measured 3 x 3 joint
  table (the two classifications of one person are fitted jointly and are not
  independent).
- H1 and H2 are two separate registered claims, each calibrated at 5%. 'Either
  claim' is not a registered quantity: even at mu = 0 it is close to the sum of
  the two rates (up to about 0.09 at spread 1.0). It is reported because a
  shared bias on both features raises both at once.
- A shared bias at mu = 1.0 puts the average null person's weight AT the detection
  threshold; the grid is a sensitivity range, not an estimate of any population.
