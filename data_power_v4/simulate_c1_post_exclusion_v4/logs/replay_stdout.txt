C1: THE GROUP COMPARISON, WITH THE ATTENTION GATE
============================================================
  one-sided Mann-Whitney on the per-person weights, alpha 0.05, Holm across H1/H2
  gate: catch failures and fast responses, one-sided, controls worse, alpha 0.05
  when the gate fires: C1 = INCONCLUSIVE
  catch block 10 trials, p_fail = 0.75 x lambda
  personal lapse rates: gamma around the group mean, SD 0.5x the mean
  fast-response cut 300 ms; P(fast | lapse) 0.4
  non-responder taste at 0.5x each threshold
  2000 studies per cell, pools of 20,000 per person type

  THE GATE'S SIMULATED DETECTION RATE IS AN UPPER BOUND: it assumes people
  lapse on catch trials at the same rate as on image trials. A catch screen
  has no pictures and one line of text, so someone skimming may well attend
  to it more. If they lapse less there, the gate is less sensitive than this.

  'ungated' is C1 read straight off the weights: it reaches
  0.158 when the controls lapse more, because attenuating the

  catch failures: the analysis that still returns an answer, at the price
  of a residual inflation of its own and of however many people it drops.

  data arrive. It works only because lapse rates differ BETWEEN people,
  so a clean catch record is evidence of a low personal rate -- if
  everyone in a group lapsed at the group mean this would select a random
  subset with the same mean and fix nothing.

      THE SUBSAMPLE REDUCES THE ARTEFACT BUT DOES NOT REMOVE IT. Where the
      controls lapse more, the ungated rate reaches 0.158 and the
      secondary's reaches 0.082, against a nominal 0.05.
      The reason is in table (a): selecting on a clean catch record leaves
      a residual lapse gap, and the power to detect that residual grows
      with the sample size and with the size of the weights. So the cells
      above nominal are the LARGE ones, not the small ones:

      THE RT RE-CHECK IS A DIAGNOSTIC, NOT A SECOND GATE. The subsample's
      own response-time test fires in 99.9% of these studies, and gating on it
      would bring the worst secondary false-positive rate to 0.001 --
      but response times do not depend on the weights, so it fires just as
      often when the effect is real, and the secondary's POWER under that
      rule is 0.000 in every cell. Registering it would amount to saying
      C1 is unanswerable whenever the groups' attention differs at all.
      So it is reported: a fired re-check says the residual contamination
      is present and the secondary's own result should be read with the
      inflation above, not as a clean test.
