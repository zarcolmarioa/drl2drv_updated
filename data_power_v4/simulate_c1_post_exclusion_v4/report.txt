WHAT THIS IS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The registered C1 numbers (c1_compact.csv, c1_cells.csv) were simulated without
the registered exclusion of people with 3 or more catch failures. This run
simulates the whole registered procedure: recruit until each group has its
target number of analysable people, then the attention check, then C1 per
feature with Holm; inconclusive if the check fired, descriptive below 20
controls; the zero-catch-failure test beside it. Nothing registered is changed.

Thresholds. Replay and validation use the thresholds the power script computes,
as the registered rates did; the registered procedure (step 3) uses the rounded
registered ones, which the analysis applies. In C1 a threshold enters only
through the taste spread, 0.5 x the upper threshold.

NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- The catch failures and fast responses come from the power script's lapse model,
  not from data; the report on the gate in the power script says why its
  sensitivity is an upper bound (people may lapse less on a catch screen).
- Recruitment is unlimited here. A smaller final N is covered by the registered
  rule for that N and by 'descriptive below 20 controls'.
- Every number is a rate over simulated studies; the Monte Carlo SE of a rate p
  is sqrt(p(1-p)/4000) here and sqrt(p(1-p)/2000) in the registered tables.
