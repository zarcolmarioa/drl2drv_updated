### Major

- [ ] **C02 · Unsupported negative-result interpretation.** **Where:** [`docs/experimental_design.md` §2.3, L149–153](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/experimental_design.md#L149-L153); [`combined_rule.csv`](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/data_power_v4/registration_v4/combined_rule.csv). **Problem/impact:** A non-support verdict does **not** “rule out” approximately half of participants responding at moderate strength. Under the specified planning alternative, failure to support remains **40.9% (colour H2) and 55.7% (fog H1)** at N=10; even at N=20 it remains 3.1% and 20.3%, respectively. No equivalence/exclusion test was registered. **Action:** Say “not supported”; give alternative-specific detection power and limits; do not infer absence without a separately justified exclusion analysis. **Blocks:** **Yes**, the wording of the preregistered inference.

- [ ] **C03 · Analysis does not verify the exact per-session frozen trial set or independently score catches.** **Where:** [`analysis_pipeline/analyse_study_v4.py` L2266–2330, L2466–2510](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/analysis_pipeline/analyse_study_v4.py); [`docs/analysis_script_plan.md` §3.1 and Part 2 A](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/analysis_script_plan.md). **Problem/impact:** The script checks 211 rows, 180 analysed answers, valid trial IDs and trial types, but not that *each* of the 211 distinct frozen trial IDs appears exactly once per session. A duplicated catch replacing another can satisfy row counts and alter exclusions; `catch_pass` is taken from incoming tidy data rather than independently checked against recorded key and expected sequence. The raw-CSV reader is explicitly unfinished. **Action:** Fail closed unless `(session_id, trial)` is unique and each complete session has exactly the frozen 211 IDs, flags and ten catches; derive/verify catch success from the recorded response and registered key sequence; exercise corrupted-input tests before freezing/versioning. **Blocks:** **Yes**, final analysis-script freeze.

- [ ] **C04 · The “5% false claims including personal tastes” wording exceeds the calibration domain.** **Where:** [`docs/experimental_design.md` §2.2, L108–115](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/experimental_design.md#L108-L115); [`simulate_power_v4_n10_20.py` `classification_rates`, L3128–3211](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/stimulus_power_pipeline/simulate_power_v4_n10_20.py#L3128-L3211). **Problem/impact:** The registered taste null is **zero-centred and symmetric by construction**. Directionally biased nonresponders are not covered by the 5% calculation. Illustratively—not as an estimate of this cohort—if a null person has classification probabilities `P(predicted)=0.20`, `P(opposite)=0.02`, a registered-style `k≥4, m≤0` rule gives **10.47%** false support at N=10; `k≥5, m≤1` gives **35.28%** at N=20. **Action:** Qualify the error guarantee explicitly as *model-conditional*; test asymmetric-null sensitivity or add independent justification for symmetry. **Blocks:** **Yes** if presented as an unconditional real-world guarantee; **no** if honestly limited to the calibrated nulls.

- [ ] **C05 · C1 operating-characteristic simulation and live exclusion procedure differ.** **Where:** [`analyse_study_v4.py` L2466–2510, L3730–3770](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/analysis_pipeline/analyse_study_v4.py); [`docs/analysis_script_plan.md` §3.3 and Part 2 D](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/analysis_script_plan.md). **Problem/impact:** The committed analysis correctly runs the C1 attention gate and group test **after** excluding people with ≥3/10 catch failures; its report explicitly acknowledges that the registered C1 simulation did **not** make this exclusion. Selection changes group sizes, attention distributions and gate/test rates. **Action:** Re-simulate the **full** selection-and-gate procedure across relevant scenarios or clearly label existing C1 rates as conditional/not the actual end-to-end operating characteristics. Do not silently alter preregistered tests. **Blocks:** **Conditional**, if existing C1 power/false-positive numbers are represented as end-to-end rates.

- [ ] **C06 · Planned consent assurance conflicts with existing immediate contact upload.** **Where:** [`docs/experiment_code_changes.md` §12, L348–374 and Part 2 item 14](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/experiment_code_changes.md); [`dpdr_experiment/src/participant-id.js` L19–29, L159–188, L276–305](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/dpdr_experiment/blob/main/src/participant-id.js). **Problem/impact:** Draft consent says closing the page at any time means “nothing will be saved,” but optional name/email are sent to a separate DataPipe destination **at identification**, intentionally surviving later abandonment. The new consent screen and text are still placeholders; development block switches also allow consent to be disabled. **Action:** Either delay contact upload or accurately disclose separately retained contact data, withdrawal rules and retention; insert ethics-approved text and enforce consent-on in production. **Blocks:** **Before launch**, and before finalising the consent procedure in the preregistration.

- [ ] **C07 · H3 angle is not an order-preserving form of the fog/colour ratio.** **Where:** [`docs/analysis_script_plan.md` §3.5 L184–190 and Part 2 F](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/analysis_script_plan.md); [`analyse_study_v4.py` L3460–3465](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/analysis_pipeline/analyse_study_v4.py#L3460-L3465). **Problem/impact:** The claim that `atan2(b_fog,b_sat)` “ranks people exactly as the ratio does” is false across quadrants: `(1,1)` and `(-1,-1)` both have ratio 1 but angles 45° and −135°; `(-1,1)` has ratio −1 but angle 135°. Angle is a vector direction, not a globally monotone ratio. **Action:** Define H3 as an angle/vector-direction *description*, retain both raw coefficients, handle wraparound/circular ordering appropriately and remove “same ranking” language. **Blocks:** **Yes** for freezing H3 interpretation; **not** for H1/H2/C1.

## B. Suspicion / methodological risk — not established as an observed failure

### Major

- [ ] **S01 · Real nonresponders may have directional visual preferences.** **Where:** [`simulate_power_v4_n10_20.py` L3128–3211](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/stimulus_power_pipeline/simulate_power_v4_n10_20.py#L3128-L3211); [`docs/experimental_design.md` §2.2](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/experimental_design.md). **Concern:** The symmetric taste null may not approximate an actual sample, especially if lower saturation/contrast is generally preferred. **Check/action:** Simulate biased null means and heterogeneous asymmetric mixtures over a plausible range; make their consequences visible alongside the registered primary analysis (see C04). **Blocks:** **Conditional** on generalising the 5% claim beyond simulated assumptions. **Unverified:** Actual null-preference distribution.

- [ ] **S02 · Repeated scenes may make trial evidence more correlated than the calibration assumes.** **Where:** [`frozen_trial_list_v4/package/trial_list.json`](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/data_trials/frozen_trial_list_v4/package/trial_list.json); [`simulate_power_v4_n10_20.py`](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/stimulus_power_pipeline/simulate_power_v4_n10_20.py). **Concern:** The 180 analysed pairs use **47 scene IDs**; **45 scenes repeat**, some up to seven times. Scene-specific preference or time-varying lapses could induce residual correlations not represented in independent trial draws, affecting fitted weights, detection rates and calibrated group rules. **Check/action:** Sensitivity simulations with scene random effects/within-scene choice dependence and serial lapses, followed by the unchanged registered classifier. **Blocks:** **Conditional** on whether reasonable dependence materially changes error rates. **Unverified:** Magnitude of dependence in human responses.

- [ ] **S03 · C1 group differences cannot isolate episodic visual memory.** **Where:** [`docs/experimental_design.md` §2.2 and recruitment/interview sections](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/experimental_design.md); [`docs/experiment_code_changes.md` §§4, 6–7](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/experiment_code_changes.md). **Concern:** Participants recall a personal episode and undergo an interview; controls imagine the condition, have different selection criteria, and do not undergo the same interview. Differences may reflect memory, prompting, expectations or selection as well as perceptual experience. Identical browser stimuli and group-dependent wording do not eliminate this. **Check/action:** State C1 as a group/framing comparison; avoid treating it as proof of perception-specific causality, or use a matched additional control. **Blocks:** **Conditional** on causal claims; not the preregistered descriptive comparison.

- [ ] **S04 · Public survey-related files may require an explicit ethics/privacy check.** **Where:** Two pseudonymised respondent-ID `.docx` files previously identified in the **public** `drl2drv_updated` repository; [`docs/analysis_script_plan.md` §2, “Inputs/Outputs”](https://github.com/zarcolmarioa/drl2drv_updated/blob/master/docs/analysis_script_plan.md). **Concern:** Pseudonyms and mental-health sampling information can remain personal data where a linkage exists. The plan keeps raw survey and ID mapping private, but public historical files require their own release justification. **Check/action:** Confirm the content, consent, ethics approval and linkage risk; remove/restrict from the public version if unauthorised. **Blocks:** **Conditional**, if public release was not approved. **Unverified:** `.docx` contents and ethics terms.

## D. What I could not verify

The two new planning files are **instructions/plans**, not proof of completed browser changes. I did **not** run the full power simulator, study simulator, analysis suite, headless browser, deployed GitHub Pages experiment, real end-to-end session, DataPipe/OSF upload or consent flow. I did not receive sample raw CSVs from the updated experiment, real participant responses, survey linkage/consent documents or ethics approval, and did not inspect the two historical `.docx` respondent files. I therefore cannot establish actual real-sample false-claim rates, scene dependence, attention-gate behaviour after exclusion, implementation completion, actual respondent privacy risk or launch readiness.

**What appears sound:** The frozen trial counts, predictor signs/left-right balance, registered count-rule arithmetic under the *specified symmetric null*, and the provenance of the already-validated **tidy-data** analysis script are consistent with the reviewed records.
