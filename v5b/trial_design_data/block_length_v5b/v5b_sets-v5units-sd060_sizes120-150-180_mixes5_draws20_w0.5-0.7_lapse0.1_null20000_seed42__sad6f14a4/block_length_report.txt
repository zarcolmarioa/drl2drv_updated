14. NOTES AND LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Power is a first estimate under the stated person model. The shift sizes, the taste
  spread, the share of responders and the lapse rate are assumptions, not estimates;
  section 9 shows how far the answer moves when they change.
* Each weight is tested on its own at alpha, uncorrected.
* The rescaled weights hold the choice rate on a typical informative trial at v4's, so
  they largely cancel the gain from larger differences: they barely separate v5units
  from sd060 by construction.
* Block order and taste changes between blocks are not modelled.
* The session length covers the two pair blocks only and is an estimate.
