12. NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Nothing here is ordered. Rows are sorted by type and couple; script 4 makes the orders.
* item_id is stable within this build: A001..., Ac01... (consistency), Ax01... (catch),
  P1_01... and P2_01... (practice before block 1 and 2). repeat_of names the original's item_id.
* 'couple' links each analysed trial to its matched partner in the other set (same scene,
  type and bin, paired in order of the type's driver).
* Every row carries pred_sat, pred_fog, a_is_left, N_corpus, N_orig and their parts, for scripts 6-9.
* candidates.csv: every candidate before (draw_*) and after (rep_*) repair; leak_by_type.csv: the
  leak by type for every candidate, and for the shipped practice and catch trials.
* BUILD_FAILED.txt is written when any check fails.
