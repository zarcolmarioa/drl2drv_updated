WHICH NUISANCES COST THE PRIMARY TEST POWER, AND 150 AGAINST 180 (nuisance_power_v5b.py): IN PLAIN WORDS

Under planning, all nuisances together cost the primary test 0.110 of power on average (half shift: 0.178). The largest costs come from fatigue building over the session (0.041); taste that differs between the two episodes (0.038); a shared preference for the duller image (0.034) (power returned when each is switched off on its own; average over both block sizes, N = 10, 15, 20 and both features). Almost nothing (less than 0.01 either way) is lost to a preference for one side of the screen, a quarter of people picking the less edited image, a quarter of people picking the more typical image and scene-specific preferences. The order differs: taste that differs between the two episodes and a shared preference for the duller image swap places under half shift (difference there +0.016, SE 0.005; beyond 2 SE); a preference for one side of the screen and a quarter of people picking the more typical image swap places under half shift (difference there +0.000, SE 0.003; within 2 SE); a quarter of people picking the less edited image and a quarter of people picking the more typical image swap places under half shift (difference there +0.001, SE 0.001; within 2 SE). The seven separate costs add up to 105% (planning) and 91% (half shift) of the total drop, so the nuisances are not additive (section 4).

Planning: going from 150 to 180 trials per block (about 4 minutes per session, 6 when responses are slow, by script 2's session estimate) changes the paired t-test's power by all defaults: +0.021 on average (+0.012 to +0.030; 4 of 6 cells ties); all defaults without fatigue: +0.034 on average (+0.012 to +0.055; 1 of 6 cells ties); all defaults, fatigue doubled: +0.007 on average (-0.000 to +0.016; 6 of 6 cells ties); all defaults, 50% recovery at the rest: +0.019 on average (+0.003 to +0.028; 3 of 6 cells ties). Fatigue itself costs 0.040 (SE 0.002) at 150 and 0.042 (SE 0.002) at 180 (power returned when it is switched off; average of 6 cells). Once fatigue is counted, the extra 30 trials per block buy at most a borderline gain: on average just above the tie limit of 0.02, but beyond a tie in only 2 of 6 cells. [ASSUMPTIONS: the effect, the nuisances and the fatigue model are planning values.]

Half shift: going from 150 to 180 trials per block (about 4 minutes per session, 6 when responses are slow, by script 2's session estimate) changes the paired t-test's power by all defaults: +0.008 on average (-0.027 to +0.032; 2 of 6 cells ties); all defaults without fatigue: +0.017 on average (+0.003 to +0.027; 3 of 6 cells ties); all defaults, fatigue doubled: -0.008 on average (-0.019 to +0.005; 6 of 6 cells ties); all defaults, 50% recovery at the rest: +0.039 on average (+0.024 to +0.059; 0 of 6 cells ties). Fatigue itself costs 0.062 (SE 0.003) at 150 and 0.068 (SE 0.004) at 180 (power returned when it is switched off; average of 6 cells). Once fatigue is counted, the extra 30 trials per block buy no meaningful gain: on average a tie (within 0.02), beyond a tie in 3 of 6 cells. [ASSUMPTIONS: the effect, the nuisances and the fatigue model are planning values.]

A preference for one side of the screen, a quarter of people picking the less edited image, a quarter of people picking the more typical image and scene-specific preferences cost almost no power.
