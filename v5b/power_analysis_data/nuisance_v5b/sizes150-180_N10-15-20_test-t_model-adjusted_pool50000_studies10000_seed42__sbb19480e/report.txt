2. WHAT WAS DONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Paired t-test (the registered primary test), power only, fixed weights,
  planning and half shift, block sizes 150 and 180, N = 10, 15, 20, both
  features; pair-specific preferences off; the registered adjusted model. Nine
  conditions: all nuisances at script 7's defaults; each of the seven switched
  off on its own (to script 7's 'off' value); no nuisances. Power returned by
  a nuisance = power with it off minus power with all defaults. Ranked by the
  unweighted average over the 12 cells. Script 7's person model, pools of
  50,000 people, 10,000 studies per cell, SE from 20 sub-pools.
  v5's 'edited looks odd' and 'block order' no longer exist in v5b: the two
  strategies ('more typical', 'less edited', a quarter each at k = 0.5 per
  unit of N) replace the first, and fatigue (lapse +0.03 and preferences
  x0.939 per 100 main trials, anchored at block 1's mean) replaces the second.
  Common random numbers: within an effect and block size, all nine conditions
  use the same random draws and the same study draws, so each recovery is a
  paired difference and its SE is that of the difference. 'Scene dependence
  off' is SD x 1e-09 rather than exactly 0, because script 7 draws the scene
  deviations only when they are on and a 0 would shift every later draw; at x
  1e-9 the deviations are numerically nothing. Switching fatigue off changes
  the lapse rate and so catch failures and who is excluded: in the fatigue-off
  and no-nuisance conditions some studies sample different people (section 6),
  which makes those two paired differences somewhat noisier.

4. DO THE SEVEN ADD UP?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Total drop = power with no nuisances minus power with all defaults. If the
  nuisances acted independently, the seven recoveries would sum to it.
  'Interacting' if the sum differs from the total by more than 2 SE and by
  more than 0.01.
  planning    total drop 0.1104 (SE 0.0031); sum of the seven 0.1155 (SE 0.0058);
              sum minus total +0.0051 (SE 0.0052); ratio 1.05 -> approximately additive
  half shift  total drop 0.1782 (SE 0.0045); sum of the seven 0.1623 (SE 0.0077);
              sum minus total -0.0159 (SE 0.0061); ratio 0.91 -> INTERACTING
  A ratio above 1 means switching nuisances off one at a time returns more
  power in total than switching them all off together (they overlap); below 1,
  that they compound (each costs more in the presence of the others).
