5. THE EPISODE-DEPENDENT STRATEGY: v5b LISTS AGAINST v5 LISTS (a threat no test can remove)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

derealization block and +k x N in the ordinary block (picks the less typical / more

no intercept): the weight difference it creates is about (k_D - k_O) x b, before

difference of the estimates). v5b's lists were built with |b| <= 0.05; v5's were not.

7. THE CHOICE OF PRIMARY TEST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rule (v5's, as amended): a test qualifies if, over the rule rows (section 4),
at N = 10, 15, 20, both sizes and both features, no more of its cells exceed 0.05 + 2 SE
(that cell's Monte Carlo SE) than chance gives in 95% of runs for a test whose false-
positive rate is exactly 0.05. Among qualifying tests, the higher mean power over planning
and half shift (all nuisances at defaults, fixed weights, same N, sizes, features) wins; within 0.02
of each other, the wilcoxon test. The original rule (no cell over the limit) is shown beside it.

9. SECONDARY: SIGN TEST ON DETECTED COUNTS WITHIN EACH BLOCK

it cannot tell a preference shared by both episodes (e.g. 'memory makes it duller',
one of the default nuisances) from a derealization effect, so with nuisances at
their defaults it claims in many studies with no derealization effect (second table).
