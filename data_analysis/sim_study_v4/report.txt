THE MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Each person: taste ~ Normal(0, taste_mult x upper threshold) on both
  features; with probability `strategy` +0.5/SD on both; responders
  (exactly round(share x N) analysable participants) add the effect.
  Personal lapse rate, catch failures and fast-response share from the
  power script's draw_attention; choices from simulate_lapsing at that
  lapse rate. A recruit with >= 3 catch failures is excluded and
  recruitment continues, so every dataset ends at its stated N.
  Word ratings, grid choices, CDS scores and session fields are
  well-formed and loosely tied to the weights; they model nothing.
  Placeholders until the experiment update is merged: the catch key
  sequence and the survey layout (one header row). State items are
  rated 0-6, as specified in the experiment changes.
