WHICH NUISANCES COST THE PRIMARY TEST POWER, AND 150 AGAINST 180 (nuisance_power_v5c.py, REGRESSION ON v5b_180): IN PLAIN WORDS

Under planning, all nuisances together cost the primary test 0.110 of power on average (half shift: 0.195). The largest costs come from fatigue building over the session (0.042); taste that differs between the two episodes (0.039); a shared preference for the duller image (0.034) (power returned when each is switched off on its own; average over 180 trials per block, N = 10, 15, 20 and both features). Almost nothing (less than 0.01 either way) is lost to a preference for one side of the screen, a quarter of people picking the less edited image, a quarter of people picking the more typical image and scene-specific preferences. The order differs: taste that differs between the two episodes and a shared preference for the duller image swap places under half shift (difference there +0.025, SE 0.006; beyond 2 SE); a preference for one side of the screen and a quarter of people picking the more typical image swap places under half shift (difference there +0.000, SE 0.003; within 2 SE); a quarter of people picking the less edited image and a quarter of people picking the more typical image swap places under half shift (difference there +0.003, SE 0.001; beyond 2 SE). The seven separate costs add up to 102% (planning) and 88% (half shift) of the total drop, so the nuisances are not additive (section 4).
