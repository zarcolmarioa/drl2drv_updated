per cell; SE from 20 sub-pools. Recruitment by slot with exclusion (3+ catch failures
of 10), as in script 7.

group a quarter pick the less typical image (-0.5 x N_corpus) and a separate quarter
the more edited image (-0.5 x N_orig), every block; mean lapse 0.1 in both groups.
