5. THE EPISODE-DEPENDENT STRATEGY: v5b LISTS AGAINST v5 LISTS (a threat no test can remove)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everyone uses a strategy that differs between the episodes: main rows, -k x N in the
derealization block and +k x N in the ordinary block (picks the less typical / more
edited image when derealized); reverse rows the other way round (information). A
strategy reaches a model WITHOUT N terms only through the leak b (N regressed on the
predictors, no intercept): the weight difference it creates is about (k_D - k_O) x b,
before logistic attenuation and lapses ('leak_shift'; 'mean_diff' is the simulated mean
difference of the estimates). The v5b lists were built with |b| <= 0.05; v5's were not.
model: adjusted (this run, v5b lists); the v5 lists use the side model, as in v5b
(the same lists and streams as v5b's run; checked in section 2).
  The adjusted model's N terms take up a strategy that is linear in N, so its mean_diff
  stays near 0.

7. THE CHOICE OF PRIMARY TEST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rule (v5's, as amended): a test qualifies if, over the rule rows (section 4),
at N = 10, 15, 20, both sizes and both features, no more of its cells exceed 0.05 + 2 SE
(that cell's Monte Carlo SE) than chance gives in 95% of runs for a test whose false-
positive rate is exactly 0.05. Among qualifying tests, the higher mean power over planning
and half shift (all nuisances at defaults, fixed weights, same N, sizes, features) wins; within 0.02
of each other, the wilcoxon test. The original rule (no cell over the limit) is shown beside it.
  Cells per test: 54; about 1.2 expected over the limit by chance, at most 3 in 95% of runs.

9. SECONDARY: SIGN TEST ON DETECTED COUNTS WITHIN EACH BLOCK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per block, each person's weight is classified with the per-set thresholds of
the adjusted model (script 1's, from the same random choosers as design.json); the claim needs
d >= 5 detections and the exact one-sided
binomial (v4's Table 4 rule). Derealization block: the secondary H1/H2.
Ordinary block: the same rule, as a reference.
Note: within one block the sign test asks whether a directional preference exists;
it cannot tell a preference shared by both episodes (e.g. 'memory makes it duller',
one of the default nuisances) from a derealization effect, so with nuisances at
their defaults it claims in many studies with no derealization effect (second table).
That is why the paired test is primary.
